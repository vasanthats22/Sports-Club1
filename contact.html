Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about contacting and joining Sports Club.

How quickly will I get a response?
We reply to all emails within 24 hours on business days. For urgent matters, call our hotline or use WhatsApp for an instant response — usually within 2 minutes.
Can I visit the club without an appointment?
Yes! You're welcome to visit during club hours (6 AM – 10 PM on weekdays, 7 AM – 9 PM on weekends). Our reception team will give you a free tour of the facilities. For a guided tour with a coach, please book a slot in advance.
Who do I contact for corporate or group bookings?
For corporate memberships, group coaching, and event bookings, email us at partners@sportsclub.com or call +1 (800) 555-0123 and ask for the corporate desk.
How do I report a facility issue or complaint?
Email help@sportsclub.com with a short description and photo (if possible). Our facility team resolves most issues within 24 hours. Urgent safety issues are handled immediately.
