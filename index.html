Questions

Frequently Asked

Answers to the questions we hear most often from new and returning members.

How do I become a member of Sports Club?
Simply choose a membership plan above, fill in the signup form, and visit our reception desk with a valid ID. Your membership card is issued on the same day. You can start using the facilities immediately.
Do you offer trial sessions before joining?
Yes — we offer a complimentary 3-day trial pass for first-time visitors. This gives you full access to gym, pool, and one group class per day. Just drop by or contact us to book your trial.
What age groups do you accept?
Our Youth Academy starts at age 6. Adult programs are open to ages 16+. There's no upper age limit — we have active members well into their 70s, with tailored programs for every fitness level.
Can I switch membership plans later?
Absolutely. You can upgrade or downgrade your membership at any time through your member portal or by speaking to reception. Any price differences are prorated for the remaining billing cycle.
Are there discounts for families or students?
Yes. We offer 20% off for verified students, and family plans save up to 30% for 3+ members. Corporate packages are also available — contact our membership team for details.
