Got Questions?

Membership FAQs

Quick answers about joining and member benefits.

Can I change my plan later?
Yes! You can upgrade or downgrade your plan anytime from your Member Dashboard. Changes take effect from your next billing cycle with no penalty.
Is there a free trial?
Absolutely! Every new member gets a 7-day free trial with full access to all facilities and one free coaching session. No credit card required to start.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes — no long-term contracts. Cancel from your dashboard or email us at members@sportsclub.com. You'll retain access until the end of your current billing period.
Do you offer family or corporate plans?
Yes — we offer discounted family packages (up to 4 members) and custom corporate memberships for teams of 5+. Email partners@sportsclub.com for a quote.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept all major credit/debit cards, UPI, PayPal, and bank transfers. Monthly and annual payment options are available with annual plans getting 2 months free.
